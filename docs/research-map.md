# GEI Research Map

## Goal

Turn the Knowledge Circle concept into a reproducible research program:

**Knowledge → Graph → Unexplored Edge → Novelty → Contradiction → Falsifiable Test → Validation**

## Public research surface

- `index.html` — visual Knowledge Circle prototype
- `benchmarks/benchmark.py` — baseline vs GEI A/B benchmark
- `theory/` — mathematical formalization
- `experiments/` — datasets and validation experiments
- `docs/research-map.md` — contribution map

## Invitation to contributors

Try to falsify GEI. Look for cases where the system identifies a supposedly novel edge that is already known, trivial, or unfalsifiable. Strong counterexamples are valuable research results.

## Evidence ladder

1. Algorithmic improvement over baseline.
2. Novelty after corpus deduplication and provenance checks.
3. Falsifiable prediction.
4. Reproducible empirical validation.
5. Independent replication.

A score increase alone is not evidence of a scientific discovery.
